Privacy

Circle Maker · last updated November 17, 2026

What stays on your device

Circle Maker runs in your browser. Your circles, settings, the secrets you have found and your Pro license key are saved only in your own browser's storage. We have no accounts and no database of users.

What leaves your device

  • The 3D engine and zip tool load from public code libraries (jsDelivr and cdnjs). Those services see a normal web request from your browser.
  • Buying Pro happens on Lemon Squeezy's checkout. Lemon Squeezy handles your payment and email address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.
  • Activating Pro sends your license key to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it, and re-checks it every few days.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies, and nothing sold to anyone.

Exports

Images, videos and 3D models you export are made in your browser and saved straight to your device.

Questions

Contact us through the email address on your Lemon Squeezy receipt.
